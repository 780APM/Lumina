# Lumina — Lumen brand kit

**Your music. Offline.**

Lumina is a standalone Windows player for local MP3, FLAC, WAV, and OGG files.
The curved L is a beam of light: simple, recognizable, and connected to the
reactive visualizer. This kit follows the selected 01 Lumen concept.

## Files

- `lumen-logo.png` — square RGBA master, with transparent outer corners.
- `lumina.ico` — Windows icon with 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, and 256 pixel sizes.
- `launch-banner.png` — wide launch artwork for a project README or announcement.
- `social-square.png` — square social announcement artwork.
- `index.html` — offline visual brand guide; open it in a browser.
- `generation-prompts.md` — exact prompts used for the generated artwork.

The app consumes the corresponding icon files under `assets/`. Promotional
artwork is not bundled into the EXE.

## Visual identity

| Role | Color |
| --- | --- |
| Lumen green | `#36F875` |
| Charcoal | `#0C1010` |
| Soft white | `#F4F6F2` |
| Secondary gray | `#B3B3B3` |

Use green for the logo and emphasis, charcoal for surfaces, and soft white for
headlines. The app's user-selectable accent does not recolor the brand mark.
Use Segoe UI on Windows; Inter or a comparable clean sans-serif works for future
marketing layouts. The generated artwork contains raster typography, not an
editable font file.

Keep a clear area of at least one-quarter of the icon's width around the mark.
Do not stretch it, add outlines, change its silhouette, or crop the green L.
For the taskbar, use the ICO; do not resize the launch banner into an icon.
The artwork is supplied in RGB for digital use, not as CMYK press-ready files.

## Copy you can use

### Headline

Your music. Offline.

### Supporting line

Local files. Living sound.

### Short announcement

Meet Lumina: a Windows music player for the tracks you already own. Local MP3,
FLAC, WAV, and OGG files. Reactive visuals. No streaming. No accounts.

### Longer description

Give your music a home. Lumina plays the audio files on your computer, brings
their titles, artists, and cover art into view, and turns the sound into a living
visualizer. Control playback, build a queue, and choose the look that suits you.
Your files, your listening session, entirely offline.

### Feature lines

- Play your local MP3, FLAC, WAV, and OGG collection.
- View embedded music metadata and cover artwork.
- Watch the sound with 2D, circular, and optional 3D visualizers.
- Adjust volume by dragging or scrolling the slider.
- Portable Windows EXE; no Python installation required for listeners.

Use a verified release/download URL when publishing. Do not advertise AAC/M4A,
cloud sync, subscriptions, streaming services, or a metadata editor: those are
not implemented in the current release.

## Production notes

The logo and campaign graphics were created with the built-in image-generation
tool, using the chosen Lumen concept as a reference. ICO/PNG size exports preserve
the generated logo. No external campaign has been published.
