## Lumen icon master
+
Use case: logo-brand. Create the finished Lumina Windows application icon based STRICTLY on option 01 LUMEN in the supplied concept board. Input image is a design reference, not a sheet to preserve. Output ONE isolated square icon, no text and no concept board. Preserve the curved, folded light-beam uppercase L silhouette of option 01: thick vertical stroke with rounded top-right cap, broad smooth elbow, and horizontal foot ending in a rounded sweep. Bright luminous green (#36f875) with a restrained darker green fold for depth. Almost-black charcoal (#0c1010) rounded-square tile, clean flat fills, crisp edges. The tile should occupy about 94% of the square canvas and the L should occupy about 62% of the canvas width and height with balanced optical centering and padding. Only the OUTSIDE of the rounded tile is genuinely transparent; retain the dark tile behind the green L. No outer shadows, no blurry glow, no extra symbols, no letters beyond the L mark, no watermark. Large bold geometry readable when reduced to 16 and 32 pixels. Produce a clean production-ready raster master with transparent outer corners; closely match the selected 01 design rather than redesigning it.
